# Architecture Oral Defense

For each capstone decision answer without notes:
1. What requirement drove it?
2. What alternative did you reject?
3. What failure does it address?
4. What security boundary exists?
5. What is the scaling unit?
6. What is the largest cost driver?
7. What happens during an AZ failure?
8. What happens during a Region failure?
9. How is it observed?
10. How is it recovered?
