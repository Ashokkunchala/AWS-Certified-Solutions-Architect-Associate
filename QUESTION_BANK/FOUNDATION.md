# Foundation Scenarios

## Q1
A workload runs on private EC2 instances and must access S3 without traversing the public internet. What architecture should be considered?

**Reasoning target:** identify private service access and distinguish endpoint types from NAT.

## Q2
An application must survive an Availability Zone failure with minimal operational work. What architectural characteristics should be present?

**Reasoning target:** multi-AZ deployment, load balancing, health checks and managed resilience.
